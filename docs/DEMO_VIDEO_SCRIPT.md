# 90-Second SpecOS Demo Video Script & Walkthrough

> **Goal:** Create an irresistible 90-second Loom or Screen Studio video that immediately demonstrates high agency, deep technical product sense, and execution velocity to founders and VPs of Product.
> **Target Recording Length:** 85–95 seconds.

---

## 🎬 Pre-Recording Checklist
1. Open `http://localhost:5173/` in a clean, full-screen browser window (Arc, Chrome, or Safari in Dark Mode).
2. Ensure browser zoom is at 100% or 110% for crisp typography.
3. Have your webcam bubble visible in the bottom-left corner with good lighting and clear audio.
4. Keep the cursor smooth and intentional.

---

## ⏱️ Second-by-Second Script & Screen Action

### 0:00 – 0:15 | The Hook & The Problem
* **Screen:** Show an empty Notion PRD or Google Doc tab for 2 seconds, then switch to SpecOS.
* **Camera:** Looking directly into camera.
* **Spoken Script:**
  > *"Most product specs in Notion or Google Docs rot within two weeks. Why? Because they’re static, they miss critical production edge cases, and engineering leads end up spending hours manually decomposing them into Linear tickets.  
  > To solve this, I reverse-engineered the speed of Linear and the modular block architecture of Notion to build **SpecOS**—a local-first AI workspace that turns a one-sentence feature intent into an executable PRD with auto-decomposed tickets in 30 seconds."*

---

### 0:15 – 0:40 | The Live Demonstration (The "Aha!" Moment)
* **Screen:** Click the **"Stripe 1-Click Guest Checkout"** preset on the SpecOS prompt bar.
* **Action:** Highlight the living block tree as it appears:
  1. Point to the **North Star KPI** banner (`+18.4% Mobile Checkout Conversion`).
  2. Scroll to the **Gherkin Acceptance Criteria** (`GIVEN... WHEN... THEN...`).
  3. Scroll to the **Data Schema Diff** showing exact SQL table migrations (`orders` table, `guest_sessions` table).
* **Spoken Script:**
  > *"Notice how the spec isn't just unstructured markdown. It generates a typed block tree: a quantifiable North Star metric, user journeys, Gherkin acceptance criteria, and exact database schema diffs so engineering has zero ambiguity before writing a line of code."*

---

### 0:40 – 0:58 | The Secret Weapon: The Synthetic Tech Lead Audit
* **Screen:** Pan or zoom into the right-hand panel: **Synthetic Tech Lead Audit**.
* **Action:** Hover over the **Concurrency** and **Idempotency** failure cards.
* **Spoken Script:**
  > *"Here is the secret weapon: the **Synthetic Tech Lead Audit**. Before an engineer even looks at this document, an adversarial agent stress-tests the feature across four failure vectors: race conditions, webhook idempotency, rate limits, and UX recovery.  
  > For guest checkout, it immediately flagged the classic double-tap race condition and prescribed client-side idempotency keys tied to cart hashes."*

---

### 0:58 – 1:15 | Linear Task Decomposition & Live UI Sandbox
* **Screen:** Scroll down to show the **Interactive Apple Pay Mockup**, click "Pay with Apple Pay", watch the biometric auth simulate, then point to the **Linear Issue Decomposition** panel.
* **Spoken Script:**
  > *"It also renders an inline interactive prototype of the feature to validate UX ergonomics in real time.  
  > Then, it automatically decomposes the entire PRD into atomic, typed Linear tickets—complete with P1 to P4 priorities, Fibonacci estimates, and acceptance criteria. With one click, I can copy the JSON directly into Linear’s sprint backlog."*

---

### 1:15 – 1:30 | The Closer (Agency & Pitch)
* **Screen:** Click **"Copy PRD"** (shows the green checkmark), switch briefly to the GitHub repo showing `docs/PRD.md` and `docs/adr/ADR-001.md`.
* **Camera:** Direct to camera.
* **Spoken Script:**
  > *"I built SpecOS with a local-first React and TypeScript architecture, zero-latency state, and documented full ADRs in the open-source repo.  
  > Previously, I drove product discovery and delivery for 120,000 students at Edwisely and automated analytics pipelines at Swiggy. I'm looking for high-velocity APM and Technical PM roles where I can bring this exact agency and execution pace.  
  > Let's connect!"*

---

## 💡 Pro-Tips for Recording
* **Pace:** Speak with crisp, calm confidence. Don't rush, but keep energy high.
* **Tool:** Use **Screen Studio** (if you have it) for automatic smooth cursor zooms, or **Loom** set to 1080p.
* **Call to Action:** Pin your LinkedIn profile and the live Vercel URL in the video description.
